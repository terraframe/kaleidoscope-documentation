- What a flood scenario represents: output from a separate flood analysis, linked to the objects at risk. The current data has two: "No Mitigation Scenario" and "Combo Plan Scenario". Confirm where the scenarios come from (DOC-ISSUES.md).
- What it is not: a real-time flood forecast, or a Kaleidoscope calculation.
- How to interpret results: "predicted to be affected under this scenario", not "will flood".
