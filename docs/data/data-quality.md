- Tracing a result back to its source record: link to [Trusting and verifying results](../concepts/verifying-results.md) rather than repeating it.
- Reporting a data problem: Geoprism Registry isn't the authoritative source, so say who to contact about a problem in the source data, and who to contact about a problem introduced in Geoprism Registry (for example, a wrong relationship). Neither is fixed in Kaleidoscope.
