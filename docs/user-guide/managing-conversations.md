# Managing conversations

Each conversation with Kaleidoscope is kept in the **Chat History** tab of the sidebar, so you can go back to it later. Kaleidoscope remembers the earlier messages in a conversation, so follow-up questions build on them.

![The chat page. The Chat History tab in the sidebar lists three conversations, each with a pencil icon and an x icon, and the New chat button is at the bottom of the sidebar.](/img/screenshots/chat-page.png)

## Starting a new conversation

Click **New chat** at the bottom of the sidebar. A new, empty conversation opens.

Start a new conversation when you change topic, so that earlier messages don't affect Kaleidoscope's answers.

## Going back to a conversation

In the **Chat History** tab, click the conversation. It opens with all its messages.

## Renaming a conversation

1. Click the pencil icon (**Edit conversation name**) next to the conversation, or double-click its name.
2. Type the new name.
3. Press Enter, or click anywhere else, to save it. To cancel instead, press Escape or click the x icon (**Cancel rename**).

## Deleting a conversation

1. Click the x icon (**Delete conversation**) next to the conversation.
2. Click **Delete** to confirm. The conversation is deleted, and this can't be undone.

## Clearing a conversation

In a long conversation, earlier questions can confuse Kaleidoscope's answers to later ones. To start the current conversation afresh, click the eraser icon (**Erase conversation**) to the left of the message box. This erases the conversation's messages and clears Kaleidoscope's memory of them, so your next question is answered on its own.

The conversation stays in **Chat History**. To keep the old conversation and start another, click **New chat** instead.

:::info
Your chat history is stored in your browser's local storage. It's only available in the browser you used, and clearing your browser's data deletes it.
:::
