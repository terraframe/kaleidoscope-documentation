# Welcome to Kaleidoscope

Kaleidoscope is the GeoAI solution for viewing and exploring authoritative data, brought into AI by [Geoprism Registry](https://docs.geoprismregistry.com/version/v2.0.0/introduction). It was built for the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE), so that USACE can use its data with GeoAI. You ask questions in plain language, and Kaleidoscope answers from that data.

Kaleidoscope's main use is finding and exploring sites to assess them for flood risk mitigation. One of its goals is to help produce reports for site analysis and selection, and what it does may grow beyond that over time.

Kaleidoscope is for USACE planners, emergency managers, water resource managers and analysts who need answers without writing queries or combining GIS layers themselves. It's [open source](https://github.com/terraframe/kaleidoscope), but tailored to USACE's data and needs.

![The Kaleidoscope chat page. Conversations are listed on the left, under Chat History. Questions and answers fill the middle of the page, and each answer has a View on Map button. Questions are typed in the message box at the bottom.](/img/screenshots/chat-page.png)

On the chat page, you type a question in the message box at the bottom and click **Send**. Kaleidoscope's answer appears in the conversation, sometimes with a table of results, and **View on Map** shows the objects behind it on a map. Your conversations are listed on the left under **Chat History**. To start a new conversation, click **New chat** at the bottom of the sidebar. See [Managing conversations](../user-guide/managing-conversations.md).

## What you can do with Kaleidoscope

* **Ask questions in plain language.** For example, "What built infrastructure is in the study area?" or "Which structures are at risk of flooding in this scenario?"
* **See the answers on a map.** Click **View on Map** on an answer to show the objects behind it on a map and in a results table.
* **Explore how places and assets are connected.** The graph visualizer shows the objects connected to the one you select. See [Exploring connections](../user-guide/exploring-connections.md).
* **Inspect the underlying data.** Open any object to see its attributes and its code, which identifies its record in Geoprism Registry's knowledge graph. See [Inspecting an object](../user-guide/inspecting-an-object.md).
