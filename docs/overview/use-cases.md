# Use cases

This page describes what Kaleidoscope is used for. The example questions show the kinds of questions it can answer. What you can ask at any time depends on the data in the current [data release](../data/data-releases.md).

## Finding and assessing sites for flood risk mitigation

Kaleidoscope's main use is finding sites and exploring what's around them, to assess them for flood risk mitigation. For example, you can see what's inside a project area, which structures are at risk of flooding under different flood scenarios, and which mitigation measures a plan includes.

Example questions:

* "What built infrastructure is in the study area?"
* "Which structures are at risk of flooding in the No Mitigation Scenario?"
* "How many structures are at risk in the No Mitigation Scenario compared with the Combo Plan Scenario?"
* "What is the total value of the structures at risk of flooding in the No Mitigation Scenario?"
* "What mitigation measures are in the project area?"

## Checking where the data came from

Each object in Kaleidoscope's data records the source it came from and the organization responsible for it. You can ask Kaleidoscope about this provenance, to decide how far to rely on an answer.

Example questions:

* "What is the provenance of this data?"
* "Which organization is responsible for the structure data?"

## Reports for site analysis and selection

:::info
This is the direction Kaleidoscope is heading. It isn't available yet.
:::

One of Kaleidoscope's goals is to help produce reports for site analysis and selection, drawing on the same data you explore in the app. What Kaleidoscope does may grow beyond this over time.
